Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Property Appointment Manager ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Property Appointment Manager is designed as an offline-first productivity tool. We collect minimal data necessary to provide scheduling and backup functionality:

  • Local CRM Data: Property titles, locations, inspection time slots, client names, contact numbers, and appointment notes entered directly into the app. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Google Account Information: When you connect your Google Account for cloud backups, we obtain your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture) to identify your connected backup destination.

Google Drive & Google API Services Compliance

Property Appointment Manager requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This restricted permission only allows our app to create and manage files that the app itself uploads inside the dedicated folder Property Appointment Manager Backups.

Our application cannot access, read, or modify your personal photos, documents, spreadsheets, or any other files stored in your Google Drive.

2. Limited Use Policy Disclosure

Property Appointment Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We never sell your Google user data to advertisers or third parties.
  • We never use your Google Drive data for advertising or marketing profiling.
  • We never transfer your data to external servers outside your personal Google Drive account.

3. How Your Data Is Stored & Protected

Your database snapshots are serialized into encrypted JSON files and transmitted directly over HTTPS to Google's official Google Drive API endpoints (https://www.googleapis.com). Backups remain strictly within your personal Google account storage.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your stored data:

  • Auto-Retention Policy: You can configure the app to automatically delete older backups, keeping only the last 3, 5, 10, or 20 snapshots.
  • Manual Snapshot Deletion: You can delete any backup snapshot from Google Drive directly within the app's Cloud Backup modal with 1 tap.
  • Unlinking Account: You can disconnect your Google Account at any time from the app settings, which removes all stored auth tokens immediately.
